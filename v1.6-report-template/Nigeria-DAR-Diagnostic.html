How to read this report

Evidence classes

M Measured — a number was recorded  D Documented — a citable artefact  J Judged — assessor statement without artefact  G Gap — searched for and not found. The class is derived from the recorded value, never chosen.

Levels & bands

Every indicator carries a level, L1–L5. A pillar band is the plain mean of its levels: Nascent <1.8 • Emerging <2.6 • Established <3.4 • Advanced <4.2 • Transformative ≥4.2. A band in (parentheses) rests mostly on judgement or gaps.

Pillars

A1 Agriculture & need • C1 Connectivity & access • C2 Data & digital public infrastructure (DPI) • C3 Policy & safeguards • C4 People & institutions • E1 Innovation, solutions & emerging tech • O1 Outcomes & inclusion.

Use-case areas

ADV advisory & extension • SMF smart farming • MKT market linkage & pricing • SCM supply chain • FIN financial services • AGI agricultural intelligence (AI-enabled analytics and decision systems).

Source tiers

T1 official statistics & international databases • T2 peer-reviewed & flagship reports • T3 government legal and policy artefacts • T4 reputable grey literature • T5 news/vendor material (admitted only in the register, for existence facts). Tiers are reported, never weighted.

Playbook tags & IDs

Researched narrative · desk draft — cites scored rows; sets no level

Nigeria’s need profile is a productivity story before it is a technology story. A third of the workforce (33.5%, 1.3) produces cereal yields of 1,558 kg/ha (1.2) — less than half of what irrigated systems in the region demonstrate — while one person in five is undernourished (8.1). The food production index (1.4) reads well but is stale (2022) and routed for refresh. The desk pass could not verify five of the ten need metrics — post-harvest loss, market access, credit access, climate-smart adoption, and women’s landholding — which are recorded as gaps and drive the ledger (§9), not silently omitted. Two A1 additions are proposed to the census (spec §13.2): cereal import dependency and irrigation coverage, both machine-fetchable.

Researched narrative · desk draft — cites scored rows; sets no level

The landscape is crowded and discontinuous: the two largest programs (GES, ABP) are closed, the national digital-agriculture strategy has waited unadopted since 2020, and NIDAP — the harmonization vehicle — is early. Farmer registration is the register's structural overlap: GES (closed) enrolled 4.5M+, ABP (closed) 4M+, and private platforms report millions more — yet the national registry (indicator 3.3) records ~279k farmers, under 1% of farming households. Enrollment has happened repeatedly in program silos; a registry has not.

8Strategic questions

feeds Step 2 agenda
  1. Blocked cell AGI · 3.11

    Agricultural data interoperability standards are Absent (3.11, L1). Which institution gets the standards mandate, and what moves 3.11 to Adopted before — not after — NIDAP scales?

    Precedent India’s AgriStack established national agricultural data standards and farmer-registry rails on digital ID — operating at national scale. T3

  2. Blocked cell AGI · 4.5

    No agricultural data governance framework exists (4.5, Absent) while AI-enabled public agro-systems launch. Who owns farm-level data, under what consent regime, given responsible-AI safeguards are Present but narrow (7.12)?

  3. Prerequisite 3.3 · register overlap

    GES and ABP enrolled millions in now-closed silos; the national registry holds ~279k (<1% of farming households). Consolidate, federate, or rebuild — what is the registry path that FIN and AGI both depend on?

    Precedent Rwanda’s Smart Nkunganire consolidated input-subsidy registration onto one national platform. T4

  4. Universal prerequisite 2.9

    Rural electricity access is 23.5% — Present, narrowly. Which use cases can sequence inside today’s rural power envelope, and where must electrification lead before digital service rollout?

    Precedent Pay-as-you-go solar bundled with agricultural services is documented at scale across East and West Africa. T4

  5. Partial cell ADV · 5.5

    Digital extension capability is Present (narrow): platforms exist, the extension ratio does not support them. What pairing of community-based advisor (CBA) models and digital advisory moves ADV from Partial to Ready?

    Precedent Digital Green’s video-mediated extension carries independent impact evaluations; Ethiopia’s 8028 farmer hotline operates nationally. T2

  6. Unverified prerequisite 5.7

    Does FMAFS have a staffed, funded digital/AI unit? The desk pass could not verify it (5.7) — a first-mission question, because both delivery-risk flags hang on it.

  7. Matrix · thin enablers

    Every non-blocked column is Partial for the same reason — thin enablers (layer mean 2.32 vs Foundation 2.67). Which two or three enabler investments unlock the most columns at once?

Method — DAMM v1.6 in five lines

  1. Every indicator gets a value, a source, and a year — or a recorded gap. No level without a recorded value.
  2. The value sets the evidence class: number → Measured · citable artefact → Documented · neither → Judged · looked-for-and-not-found → Gap. Derived, never chosen.
  3. Pillars report a band and their evidence composition. No weights, no overall score, no stage.
  4. Prerequisites bind on presence only; a failed prerequisite blocks its use-case column, not a global stage.
  5. Source tiers (T1–T5) are reported, never weighted. Researched narrative cites scored rows; no narrative claim sets a level.

Automated QC: 8/8 renderer checks passed (Layer 2, DAMM-v1.6-QC-Protocol.md); a failed check blocks the render. Human gates — G1 machine-pass confirmation, G2 peer review of prerequisite and Judged rows, G3 TTL sign-off — pending: this is a desk pass.

Prohibitions: no cross-country ranking · no band as a project development objective (PDO), disbursement-linked indicator (DLI), or disbursement condition · no automatic financing decisions · no public claim before human review.
