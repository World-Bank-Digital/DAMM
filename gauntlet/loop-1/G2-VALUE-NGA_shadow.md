# Did Gate 2 earn its share? — Nigeria

The same comparison against the same verified assessment, before and after the second review. `NGA_shadow` is the first pass alone; `NGA_shadow_g2` is the first pass with Gate 2's surviving findings applied.


## What it cost

| | first pass | Gate 2 | Gate 2 as a share |
|---|---|---|---|
| cost | $15.32 | $6.14 | 29% of the two passes together |
| rows | 57 | 36 | 63% of the register |
| minutes | 25 | 12 | |

Decision G3 reserves 15% of the country budget for Gate 2 — $75 of $500. It spent **$6.14**, which is 8% of that allocation.

**3 rows** had their level changed by the second review (2 filled, 0 withdrawn, 1 relevelled). That figure, not the upheld count, is what the 15% buys.

## Reading this

Two cautions before this table is used to decide anything.

**Moving toward the verified assessment is not the only good outcome.** The verified assessment is the best available answer, not the truth, and Gate 2 can be right where it disagrees with it — the hand-run gauntlet's own Gate 2 changed rows that had already passed Gate 1. A row moved away from the verified level is a row to read, not a row to count against Gate 2.

**Two independent runs of the first pass alone differ on about four rows.** A change of one or two in this table is inside that noise. Only a change larger than that is evidence about Gate 2 rather than about run-to-run variance.
