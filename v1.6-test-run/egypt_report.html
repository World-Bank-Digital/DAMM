1 · The diagnostic in brief

Egypt enters this diagnostic with the rails largely built and the returns still pending. Connectivity & access (3.50) and Policy & safeguards (3.75) read Advanced — rural coverage, affordability, a data protection law, digital ID, a national AI strategy with an agriculture component. Data & DPI (3.00) and the innovation ecosystem (3.09) read Established. The two pillars that lag are the two the roadmap exists for: People & institutions (2.57) and Outcomes & inclusion (2.43), both Emerging.

The agriculture pillar sharpens the point. Egypt's intensive irrigated system posts value-added per worker at L4 and cereal yields at L5 — the production system is strong. The need concentrates in equity and resilience: women hold 5.2% of agricultural land (L1, and the figure dates from 2015), undernourishment sits at 9.4%, and post-harvest losses of 10–30% are documented but never measured into a level.

The single sharpest number in the assessment: rural smartphone ownership is 13.9% — observed in 2015 — while national mobile ownership reads L5. The distance between those two readings is the adoption gap the whole roadmap has to close, and the reading that matters is a decade old. Six of the seven binding constraints below sit on the demand side: farmer digital literacy (L1), gender balance in the digital-agriculture workforce (3.9%), local-language services, climate-advisory reach, women's landholding.

The readiness matrix clears five of six use-case columns as Ready. The exception is Agricultural intelligence: Partial — interoperability standards and the agricultural data governance framework are both present only narrowly (L2). A national ag-data or AI play would run ahead of its rails; the two L2 prerequisites are the cheap, high-leverage fixes.

The evidence ledger is where this assessment ages: 15 readings are stale, and they are concentrated exactly where the constraints are — rural devices (2015), women's land (2015), gender workforce (2018). Egypt's refresh list is short, specific, and should precede drafting: the roadmap's priority chapters currently stand on its oldest numbers.
