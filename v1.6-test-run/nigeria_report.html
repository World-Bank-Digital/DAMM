1 · The diagnostic in brief

This is a machine-first diagnostic: 28 of 57 indicators carry session-verified evidence (15 Measured, 13 Documented), 8 are marked as explicit judgements, and 21 are recorded gaps — looked for and not found, weighted nothing, listed for the mission. Three pillar bands print in parentheses because judged-plus-gap readings outnumber evidenced ones. Read those as hypotheses with a work plan attached.

The agriculture pillar tells an extensification story. The food production index reads L4 (119.9, 2022) while cereal yield reads L2 (1,558 kg/ha), agricultural employment is a third of the workforce, and undernourishment is 19.9%. Output has grown by adding land and labour, not productivity — which is precisely the gap digital advisory, input finance and market access exist to close. The impact case for a Nigeria DAR writes itself from this pillar; almost nothing else about it is yet measured.

The sharpest structural finding: Policy & safeguards (2.86, Established) is running ahead of Data & DPI (2.17) and People & institutions (2.00). The NDPA is in force and actively enforced (1,368 compliance notices in 2025); a National AI Strategy names agriculture as a priority sector; NIN digital ID covers ~57% and is climbing under a 2026 full-coverage directive. But the National Digital Agriculture Strategy remains a 2020 draft that was never adopted, there are no agricultural data interoperability standards, and no agricultural data governance framework. Nigeria has laws ahead of rails, and a strategy shelf with an ownership vacuum.

That inversion becomes concrete in the readiness matrix: Agricultural intelligence is Blocked — the only blocked column — by the two absent prerequisites (3.11, 4.5), in the same year the government launched an AI-powered national agro-productivity system. The national leapfrog gap reads 0.0; the use-case grain catches the fragility the aggregate hides. The AI push is real, and it is standing on data rails that do not exist yet.

Financial services is the nearest-ready column: agri-fintech rails operate (PSBs, Crop2Cash, ThriveAgric; $865M digital lending across sectors in 2025), female account ownership is 52%, NIN supports KYC. What holds it at Partial is the farmer registry — launched 2025, NIN-linked by design, but at ~279k farmers it covers under 1% of farm households. Scaling the registry is the single move that unblocks targeted credit, subsidy delivery, and eventually the ag-intelligence column.

Two foundations need watching: rural electrification at 23.5% sits just above the universal-gate floor — power, not spectrum, is the binding rural constraint; and rural-specific coverage and smartphone figures are simply unpublished. The verify list of 24 items is not a footnote — it is the mission agenda, and on this run it is the largest single output of the diagnostic.
